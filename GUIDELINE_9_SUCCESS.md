## 🎮 **HOW TO EXPERIENCE THE AI FEATURES**

1. **Launch Application**: `gradle run` - Application starts with AI ready
2. **Open AI Chat**: Click "Show/Hide AI Chat" or use the conversational interface on the right
3. **Try Quick Suggestions**: Click any suggestion button for instant AI interaction
4. **Ask Questions**: Type natural language questions like:
   - "How do I learn Python programming?"
   - "Explain recursion in simple terms"
   - "What's the best way to study for exams?"
   - "Help me understand machine learning"
5. **Switch Modes**: Use the mode selector to get specialized responses
6. **Continue Conversation**: AI maintains context across multiple messages
